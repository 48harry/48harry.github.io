Preparing to do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Scheduling time to do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Making a to-do list for the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Telling people you're going to do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Messaging friends who may or may not be doing the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Writing a banger tweet about how you're going to do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Hating on yourself for not doing the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Hating on other people who have done the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Hating on the obstacles in the way of doing the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Fantasizing about all of the adoration you'll receive once you do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Reading about how to do the thing isn't doing the thing.<br/>
Reading about how other people did the thing isn't doing the thing. Reading this essay isn't doing the thing.<br/>
The only thing that is doing the thing is doing the thing.<br/><br/>
