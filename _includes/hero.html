{%- comment -%} Home hero, a poster: the newsletter's keywords, day by day, fill the screen as a wall, and the wordmark sits across the bottom, edge to edge. hero.js builds the wall from the JSON below: a lens that brings the words to full ink and lights the one under the pointer, a ring of ink that goes out from the wordmark's ␣ on load and with the space bar, and the scroll that pulls the two words apart. Without JS it is the wordmark and the line on the plain canvas. Corpus: the 90 newest posts. Newsletter issues give their `keywords` (front matter), each with the id of the first item (### heading and what follows it) that mentions it, so a keyword links straight to that item; an issue without keywords gives the start of each item's heading. Other posts give their title. If hero.js can't load, its onerror hands the navbar its wordmark back (it waits for .hero-passed). {%- endcomment -%}
